# NOTE**COMPLETE** :star:
_Handwritten notes, PDFs, Markdown, Chinese tools and AI study, on every screen_

## What it is
A local-first notebook app for **handwriting, PDFs, Markdown notes and study**, with built-in Chinese tools and your own AI. Write with a pen or finger, edit Markdown side by side, search everything, listen to audio, and **share** a note live.
-> Everything runs on your device. No account, no upload.
-> Download links are in the ==Notes panel== beside these pages ((press N)).

>> [sky] WRITE: pen with pressure, highlighter, shapes, tape, rich text & math || [amber] MARKDOWN & CHINESE: split editor, Pinyin IME, stroke order & translate
>> [teal] STUDY & AI: flashcards, quizzes, handwriting from Markdown & MCP || [pink] SHARE: live sessions & phone as a drawing tablet
>> [purple] READ: PDFs, Word, Excel, HTML, archives & notebooks || [coral] AUDIO: global MP3 player & ink-synced recordings

## Also opens · exports
>> [purple] OPENS: PDFs, images, HTML, Markdown, Word, Excel, CSV, PlantUML, archives, **Noteful** (.noteful), **Goodnotes** and **Notability** notebooks, plus MP3/MP4 media || [coral] EXPORTS: PDF, SVG, images, .noteful, Markdown and Anki study decks

# WRITE, TYPE // AND MARKDOWN
_Handwriting, rich text boxes and split-view Markdown_

>> [sky] PEN &amp; SHAPES: pressure, smoothing, and hold-to-snap lines, arrows, rectangles, circles, triangles and stars || [amber] HIGHLIGHTER &amp; TAPE: blends across PDF words; tape hides answers until tapped
>> [teal] TEXT BOXES: rich styles, live LaTeX math, hold/drag in Text mode to move without switching to Select || [pink] MARKDOWN EDITOR: resizable split preview, independent Edit/View zoom, width &amp; 3-way alignment

## Make it yours
- **Quick menu**: hold ==Shift S==, use a pen's side button or hold two fingers still. Hold any tool to customize or reorder it.
- **Tool bar** on the top, bottom, left or right (View → Tool bar), with or without names; side-by-side 2-up pages and Dark pages (==D==).
- **Prefabs** (==Shift X==) & **Tables** (==Shift Y==): editable graphs, charts, CPU timelines, trees, UML diagrams and stroke-order sheets; double-click medallions in Markdown to edit directly.

# CHINESE // AND TRANSLATE
_Built-in Pinyin IME, stroke order, lookup and universal translation_

>> [amber] CHINESE IME: type Hanzi or tone-marked Pinyin with mid-syllable tone digits, dead-key accents, fuzzy typo suggestions, 2D arrows and Space / Enter / Shift+1–9 || [sky] PINYIN OVERLAY: toggle live tone-coloured Pinyin over characters from View or the toolbar
>> [teal] LOOK UP &amp; STROKES: right-click any character for meanings ((ES | EN)), radical &amp; part meanings, audio, animated stroke order &amp; practice, Favorites and HSK / CC-CEDICT dictionaries || [purple] UNIVERSAL TRANSLATE: right-click any highlighted text to translate into favorite languages ((Español, English, 中文 + 33 more)) and **Replace** in place

- Right-click any Chinese character or selection in pages or Markdown to look up, listen, translate or open its Stroke order editor.
- Repeat toggles on stroke animations stay synced with Play/Pause without interrupting playback.

# READ, FIND // AND AUDIO
_Documents, unified search and built-in media playback_

## Find & Library
- **Find** (==Ctrl F==): searches typed text, PDF text, on-device handwriting OCR and audio transcripts, plus full-content search across your library on Home.
- **Library & Cloud**: open local folders as sidebar groups, connect **Google Drive**, **Dropbox**, **Internet Archive** or **GitHub**, compare newer versions, and filter by type, date, source, size, audio or tags.

## Audio & Media
- **Global MP3 player**: open any `.mp3` from a folder, file picker or cloud to play it directly from the top bar ((headset mute, back/play/forward, seek bar, speed)) without closing your note; `.mp4` videos open in your computer's player.
- **Synced recordings**: record or import audio into a note so ink replays as it was written; transcribe locally with Whisper or Voxtype, click any word to seek, and revise with AI while keeping timings.

!stat 30 seconds back or forward in note audio: ==J== and ==L==, ==K== to play
