# NOTE**COMPLETE** :star:
_Notes, documents and study, on every screen_

## What it is
A notebook app for **handwriting, PDFs and Markdown notes**, with study tools and your own AI. Write with a pen or a finger, type notes beside the pages, search everything, and **share** a note live.
-> Everything runs on your device. No account, no upload. Opens **Noteful** notebooks too.
-> Download links are in the ==Notes panel== beside these pages.

>> [sky] WRITE: pen with pressure, highlighter, shapes, tape, text || [amber] READ: PDFs, images, HTML and notebooks
>> [teal] STUDY: flashcards, quizzes and chat with your AI tool || [pink] SHARE: notes with people, a phone as a drawing tablet

## Also opens · exports
>> [purple] OPENS: PDFs, images ((PNG, JPEG, WebP)), HTML pages, Markdown and text files || [coral] EXPORTS: pages as PDF, SVG or images, the notes as Markdown, study decks for Anki

# SEARCH · STUDY // LISTEN
_Everything in a note, findable and learnable_

## Search everything
- **Find** (==Ctrl F==) searches a note's typed text, the text of its PDF pages, its handwriting and its recordings' transcripts, and lists the matches by page.
- Handwriting the app wrote, like this note, is searchable at once: search for tablet. Your own handwriting becomes searchable with **Recognize text** (OCR).
- Recognition and transcripts run on this device.

## Study with your AI tool
-> Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity or OpenCode, with your own subscription.
>> [teal] FLASHCARDS: decks from your pages, with Ask AI on any card || [amber] QUIZZES: from a page range, with hints from the page and a dispute button
* Export every deck and quiz to **Anki**.
* Chat about the page you are on, and keep answers in a **scratchpad**.
* **New note with AI**: describe a topic and it is written as a handwritten note.
* A **note server (MCP)** lets agents read your notebooks and write real handwriting.

## Listen along
- Recordings play with your ink: strokes light up as they were written.
- Transcripts on this device, with word timings, searchable and exportable.

## Touch and stylus
- Two fingers scroll and zoom, and a flick keeps gliding; tap with ==two fingers to undo==, three to redo.
- While a stylus is in use, one finger scrolls; put it away and a finger draws.
- **Quick menu**: hold ==Z==, press a pen's side button, or hold two fingers still. Tap a tool for its colours and widths; hold one to move it.
- Pen pressure turns on or off in the Pen's settings. Scroll past the last page for a **new page**.

-> Click a link with Pan or Select. The user guide is in the **Notes** panel ((press N)).
