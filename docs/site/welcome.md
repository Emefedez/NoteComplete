# NOTE**COMPLETE** :star:
_Notes, documents and study, on every screen_

## What it is
A notebook app for **handwriting, PDFs and Markdown notes**, with study tools and your own AI. Write with a pen or a finger, type notes beside the pages, search everything, and **share** a note live.
-> Everything runs on your device. No account, no upload. Opens **Noteful** notebooks too.
-> Download links are in the ==Notes panel== beside these pages ((press N)).

>> [sky] WRITE: pen with pressure, highlighter, shapes, tape, text || [amber] CALCULATE: formulas, graphs and tables
>> [teal] STUDY: flashcards, quizzes and chat with your AI tool || [pink] SHARE: notes with people, a phone as a drawing tablet
>> [purple] READ: PDFs, Word, Excel, HTML and notebooks || [coral] LISTEN: recordings that follow your ink

## Also opens · exports
>> [purple] OPENS: PDFs, images ((PNG, JPEG, WebP)), HTML pages, Markdown and text files, Word, OpenDocument, Excel and CSV || [coral] EXPORTS: pages as PDF, SVG or images, the notes as Markdown, study decks for Anki
- A PDF or an image becomes a note to write on. Word, Excel and text files open as Markdown you can **Edit**; Save writes a copy and never changes the original.
- **Export** is in File, and the **...** menu of any note in Home, Google Drive or Internet Archive exports it without opening it first. The **?** explains each format.
- NoteComplete is in your system's **Open with** menus, and the default for its own files.

# WRITE // AND DRAW
_Pen, highlighter, shapes and tape, the same everywhere_

>> [sky] PEN: pressure, smooth strokes, and **shapes from the pen**: hold a line, arrow, rectangle, circle, triangle or star still and it becomes clean || [amber] HIGHLIGHTER: blends like real ink, and drags across PDF text to highlight whole words
>> [teal] TAPE: hides what you want to learn; lift it with a tap || [pink] GRAB: draw a box to copy its text, ask AI, or erase everything in it

## Make it yours
- **Quick menu**: hold ==Shift S==, use a pen's side button or hold two fingers still. Hold any item for its colour and width, or to hide it.
- **Tool bar** on the top, left or right (View → Tool bar), with or without names.
- Each tool remembers its colour and width; widths are yours, 0.5 to 100 px.
- **Pen hover preview** (View menu): a ring shows where an Apple Pencil will draw. Android devices already show their own.

## Pages
- Add, insert or resize pages, or use an **infinite canvas**. Type with the Text tool and move any item with Select.

- **Math in text boxes**: the ==Sum== button in the text format bar makes a box LaTeX, typeset on the page (fractions, powers, roots, Greek letters, calculus), or write `$...$` among words. A **math keypad** types it for you and replaces the keyboard on phones.
- **Edit a formula in place**: tap a numerator or an exponent to change it.
- **Graphs** (==Shift X==): plot any function of x, several at once in their own colours, with the range, grid and numbers you want. Select one and choose **Edit graph**.
- **Tables** (==Shift Y==): type in a grid, Tab adds rows, and `$...$` in a cell is math.
- **Fact check** (==Shift F==) has your AI read a page for wrong numbers, dates and calculations.

# READ // AND FIND
_Documents of every kind, one search_

## Find (Ctrl F)
- Searches typed text, PDF text, your handwriting and the transcripts of recordings, and lists matches by page.
- **Recognize text** (Pages menu) reads your own handwriting on this device, in Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Catalan and Chinese. **Quality: High** reads each page several ways and lets them vote.

## Your library (Home)
- Notes you open stay on this device. **Filters** by type, date, source, size and tags, **sort**, favourites, folders, and a list or card view.
- **Google Drive**: search folders **and** notes by name (All Drive searches everywhere), queue a batch to download, and check for a newer version.
- **Internet Archive**: add any archive.org item as a repository and browse its files.
- **Notes panel** (==N==): Markdown notes beside any document, kept with it.

# LISTEN // ALONG
_Recordings that follow your ink_

- **Record or import** audio into a note. Strokes light up as they were written, and you can dim what was not written yet.
- **Transcripts on this device**: Whisper in the browser, or Voxtype on the desktop. Every word has its time: click a word to jump there; **Follow audio** keeps the text in step.
- **Edit** a word, or **Revise with AI** to fix misheard terms and punctuation without losing the timings.
- **Text from part of a recording**: the scissors pick a segment and give only its text.
- Search the transcript with Find, and export it as text, SRT, WebVTT or JSON.
- Gain, voice filter and normalize improve what is transcribed.

- **Local**: people and devices on the same Wi-Fi join with a QR code, a link or a 6-digit PIN.
- **Drawing tablet**: a phone or tablet becomes a pen for the computer's note, with pressure, shapes and shared undo.
- **Guests**: follow the whole note live and write its Markdown notes with you; they draw too if you allow editing, or watch view-only.

# KEYS // AND GESTURES
_The same on a computer, a tablet and a phone_

>> [sky] TOOLS: ==V== pan, ==S== select, ==P== pen, ==H== highlighter, ==E== eraser, ==T== tape, ==R== shapes, ==W== text, ==G== grab, ==O== lock || [amber] QUICK MENU: hold ==Shift S== and let go on a tool, ==Shift + right-click==, ==Shift + pen==, a pen's side button, or two fingers held still. ==Shift T== hides the tool bar
>> [teal] THE NOTE: ==Ctrl Z== undo, ==Ctrl Shift Z== redo, ==Ctrl S== save, ==Ctrl F== find, ==N== notes, ==D== dark pages || [purple] MORE: ==Ctrl K== commands, ==Ctrl J== ask AI, ==A== audio, ==K== play, ==?== shows every key on its button
>> [coral] INSERT: ==Shift X== graph, ==Shift Y== table, ==Shift I== image, ==Shift B== blank page || [pink] STUDY: ==Shift C== flashcards, ==Shift Q== quiz, ==Shift F== fact check, ==Shift O== recognize text

## Gestures
- **Two fingers**: scroll or pinch to zoom; tap to undo, three fingers to redo; hold still for the quick menu.
- **One finger**: draws with the Pen, or scrolls while a stylus is in use. **Middle button** drags the pages with any tool.
- **Hold and drag** a tool in the bar, or in the quick menu, to rearrange it.
- **Shift** with the wheel or the arrows scrolls faster; **Ctrl** with the wheel zooms; **Ctrl + drag** moves a window, **Ctrl + right button** resizes it.

-> All the keys are in Help → Keyboard shortcuts.
