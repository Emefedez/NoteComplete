## What's new in 1.2.56

- **Hear Chinese.** A 🔊 in the look-up panel (key **S**, **Shift S** for slow) plays the word or character; each pinyin syllable plays its own tone, and Chinese right-click menus have **Listen to …**. Uses the system Chinese voice when there is one, otherwise a short online clip.
- **Translate Chinese selections.** Selecting Chinese, or written pinyin like `ni3 hao3`, shows a small bar with Translate, Look up, Stroke order and Listen (also in PDFs, text boxes and the Grab tool's **中**). Translations to Spanish or English can colour each word to match its translation, and one **ES | EN** switch sets the language everywhere. Typing "Ni" at the start of a text box is now read as pinyin.
- **Batch OCR and search inside notes.** **Home → Recent → Batch OCR** recognizes handwriting and scanned pages of many notes, PDFs and pictures in the background. A second magnifier beside the Recent search box searches the text inside every document and opens the note with Find on the match.
- **Share several notes at once.** **Home → Recent → Share** selects notes and sends them with the system share sheet, as one .zip by Wi-Fi link or QR, or saves that .zip.
- **Classroom noise in transcripts.** **Revise with AI** can flag side conversations and fragments, underlined in red, to remove or keep one by one or all at once.
- **Stroke order and Markdown.** Every dictionary meaning can be put on or taken off the page, captions wrap instead of being cut, and prefab pictures stay one pill in the Markdown editors.
- **Windows and dialogs.** Floating windows share one tray, can go over the top bar, have no maximum size and shrink from a screen edge; New note, Export, Batch transcribe, Chinese settings and File formats fit without scrolling.
- **Menus: one way and one name for each thing.** Ctrl Y redoes, the theme is one choice, duplicate Receive, Expand with AI and Insert UML entries are gone, and confusing names are clearer.
- **Fixes.** SVG export of pages with a PDF background, the first PNG/PDF export of Chinese text, **View → Pinyin over characters**, and the compare dialog when the AI rewrites a note you are editing. Also includes the fixes from 1.2.49–1.2.55 (Chinese input Space handling, Compare accents, submenu timers, prefab insert dropdown and window controls).
