## What's new in 1.2.25

- **Process states: service time before the letter.** The CPU + blocked number now sits to the left of each process letter.
- **Middle-click drag in the chart editors.** Hold the middle button and drag to scroll the process states, CPU timeline and chart canvas sideways or up and down, as on the note's pages.

Earlier versions: [changelog](https://github.com/Emefedez/NoteComplete/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md). Preview installers for Android, Linux (AppImage), macOS and Windows; SHA256SUMS.txt has the checksums. Not signed or notarized; see docs/BUILDS.md.
