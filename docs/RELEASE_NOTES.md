## What's new in 2.0.22

- **On-demand Math Adaptation (shorthand → LaTeX) & thematic selection hover bar.** Highlight math shorthand in any editable field (Notes, Markdown editor, Math Bar, text boxes) and click **Adapt math** / **Adapt block** in the new multi-row thematic hover bar or right-click menu to convert it to coherent LaTeX: prefix-over diacritics (`-^x` → `\bar{x}`, `~^X` → `\tilde{X}`, `~^=` → `\approx`), parenthesized & Omarchy-normalized exponents (`5^(-5)`, `0.82⁵`, `(1-0.82)⁽8-5⁾`), subscripts (`N_i`, `C_(5,6)` → `C_{5,6}`), Spanish/English combinations (`(8 sobre 5)` / `C(5 over 8)` → `\binom`), calculus & statistics operators (`sqrt()`, `intr()`, `lim(x->inf)`, `desv()`/`sd()`/`std()`, `var()`, `ECM()`), and annotated equations/arrows (`=(como y=7)=`, `=(propiedad)=>`, `<=(razón)=>`).
- **Searchable bilingual Math Notation & LaTeX Cheat Sheet.** Open `?` from the selection bar or Math Bar to search in English or Spanish and see custom notation, equivalent LaTeX syntax, and live KaTeX previews side by side.
- **Moodle polish: course images fixed (on by default), hide/restore courses with an eye icon, and fixed SSO password caching.** Course banners now load via `core_course_get_enrolled_courses_by_timeline_classification` + `/webservice/pluginfile.php/`, each course card has an eye button to hide/archive or restore it alongside a **Hidden (N)** toggle, and Electron 44 `console-message` credential capture is fixed so encrypted login autofill saves reliably.
- **Ask AI prompt escalation.** Ask AI now focuses on the current page first, then proactively checks nearby pages, the rest of the note via MCP, and related files/web knowledge when needed.
