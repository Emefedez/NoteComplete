## What's new in 1.2.45

- **Chinese input (pinyin and hanzi).** Type Chinese in text boxes, Notes, Ask AI, flashcard editors, quiz checks and Find without an OS keyboard (**Ctrl Shift Space** or **中** cycles Off → Hanzi → Pinyin → Off). **Pinyin** turns tone numbers (`ni3hao3`) into tone marks with no dictionary. **Hanzi** shows a candidate menu at the caret with tone-coloured pinyin and HSK levels (**Space** commits, hold **Space + 1–9** chooses, digits filter tones, **Shift** switches to Latin, full-width punctuation, phone candidate strip). Common words and HSK 1–3 come bundled offline; **HSK 4, 5, 6 and 7–9** can be downloaded and deleted in **View → Chinese input…** (or **Settings → Chinese**), alongside your own words.
- **Pinyin over characters, fonts and Find.** **View → Pinyin over characters** shows tone-coloured pinyin over Chinese in pages, Notes, documents, flashcards and AI answers (saved per note and globally), and `{汉字|hàn zì}` writes custom ruby in Markdown. Bundled Noto Sans SC keeps Chinese consistent everywhere and is embedded in PDF, PNG and SVG exports (with optional pinyin in the Export dialog). **Find** matches Chinese by pinyin as well (`nihao` or `ni hao` finds 你好).
- **Look-up, simplified/traditional conversion, vocabulary cards.** Right-click (or long-press on touch) any Chinese word or character to open the look-up panel: readings, meanings, radical and parts, related words, audio pronunciation, per-instance reading override (**Use here**), **Flashcard**, and **My words**. Convert a text box or selection between simplified and traditional with ambiguity choices. **Make vocabulary cards…** turns text into flashcards with HSK filters; Chinese decks support **Export to Anki…** (ruby, furigana, or three fields) and **Writing practice**.
- **Stroke order.** **Insert prefab → Languages → Stroke order** places animated stroke-order characters with pinyin and grids (米字格 / 田字格) on pages; play, step, show step numbers or classic 1, 1–2, 1–3 sequences, and **Practise** writing each stroke with the pen, finger or mouse. Top 3 000 characters come bundled offline; ≈ 6 500 more fetch and cache on first use.

Earlier versions: [changelog](https://github.com/Emefedez/NoteComplete/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md). Preview installers for Android, Linux (AppImage), macOS and Windows; SHA256SUMS.txt has the checksums. Not signed or notarized; see docs/BUILDS.md.
