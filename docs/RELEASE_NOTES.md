## What's new in 2.0.5

- **The ? button lets go when the cheat sheet closes.** Closing an editor's cheat sheet with its × or Escape left the ? button looking pressed until it was clicked again. It now shows pressed only while the cheat sheet is open, in the UML, chart and prefab editors, the text documents and the notes.

Detailed change history: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Emefedez/NoteComplete/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md). Preview installers for Android, Linux (AppImage), macOS and Windows; `SHA256SUMS.txt` has the checksums. Not signed or notarized; see `docs/BUILDS.md`.
