## Cell **structure**
The **cell** is the unit of structure and function of living things. Every cell has a ==cell membrane==, **cytoplasm** and [[DNA]].
* **Mitochondria** are the cell's power plants; they make ATP.
* **Ribosomes** have no membrane and are in every cell.
!end
>> [coral] ANIMAL CELL: Has centrosomes and lysosomes ((no cell wall, no chloroplasts)) || [teal] PLANT CELL: Has a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large vacuole ((no centrosomes))
## What organelles **do**
1. The **nucleus** runs the cell and stores its DNA.
2. **Ribosomes** make proteins.
3. The **Golgi** packs and ships products.
4. **Lysosomes** digest material inside the cell.

## Crossing the **membrane**
>> [sky] PASSIVE TRANSPORT: Diffusion and osmosis; matter moves from high to low concentration ((no ATP used)) || [purple] ACTIVE TRANSPORT: Matter is pumped from low to high concentration ((uses ATP))
