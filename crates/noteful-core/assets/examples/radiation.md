### Hotter = shorter
Every object glows. The hotter it is, the more it emits and the shorter its waves.
> [sky] Wien's law: λ_{max} ≈ 2898 / T ((Sun: 0.5 µm · Earth: 10 µm))
> [purple] Stefan-Boltzmann: E = σT^4 ((σ = 5.67 × 10^{-8} W m^{-2} K^{-4}))
!end
>> [coral] SHORTWAVE: comes from the Sun ((0.2 – 4 µm · UV + visible)) || [sky] LONGWAVE: leaves the Earth ((4 – 100 µm · infrared))
