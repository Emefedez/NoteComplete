        Google Drive

        See your Noteful library here. NoteComplete only reads Drive; it never uploads, edits or deletes files.

        1. In Noteful, back up or export your notebooks to a Google Drive folder.
        2. Choose Connect Google Drive and allow read-only access in the Google window.
        3. Open that folder below. It reopens automatically next time, and opened notes are kept on this device.
        Connection setup

        The app owner must enable the Drive API and register this website’s origin in Google Cloud. Enter the public client ID, never a client secret.

          Dropbox

          Open notes kept in Dropbox. NoteComplete only reads Dropbox; it never uploads, edits or deletes files.

          1. In Noteful (or any app), back up or export your notebooks to a Dropbox folder.
          2. Choose Connect Dropbox and allow access in the Dropbox window.
          3. Open that folder below. It reopens automatically next time, and opened notes are kept on this device.
          Connection setup

          The app owner creates a Dropbox app with the files.metadata.read and files.content.read permissions. Enter its public app key, never the app secret.

                                              File formats
                                              
  
                                              A note is saved in one of two formats. Export makes other files from it.
                                              
  
                                              
    
                                              KeepsUse it for
                                              
    
      
                                              NoteComplete project
                                              .nfedit                                              		Your edits (with undo history), Markdown notes, recognized handwriting text, added audio recordings, flashcard decks, quizzes and scratchpad. Saving with Ctrl S keeps everything; exporting .nfedit lets you strip PDFs, recordings, history, notes or study sets with live sizes.Your notes in NoteComplete: full backup or slim shareable copy.
                                              
      
                                              Noteful
                                              .noteful                                              		Pages, ink, PDFs, images and text, as Noteful stores them. No undo history; notes and study sets stay in NoteComplete.Notes you also open in Noteful on the iPad.
                                              
                                                  
  
                                              
  
                                              File → Import, export and share… makes other files and never changes your note:
                                              
  
                                                
    
                                              • NoteComplete project (.nfedit) choose exactly what to bundle (edit history, embedded PDFs, note audio, added recordings, Markdown notes, handwriting/OCR text, quizzes, flashcards & scratchpad) before saving.
                                                • 
    
                                              • Noteful file a .noteful copy. No undo history.
                                                • 
    
                                              • Noteful + Markdown the .noteful and its notes as a .md file.
                                                • 
    
                                              • Study material (.ncstudy) export or share flashcards, quizzes and scratchpad on their own, or bundle them inside .nfedit.
                                                • 
    
                                              • Pages as SVG, PDF or images · study decks for Anki. File → Import… brings any of these back, plus quizzes from Quiz Toolkit.
                                                • 
  
                                              
  
                                              Archives (.zip, .tar, .tar.gz/.tgz, .gz) open to show what they hold, each file checked (CRC-32 for zip and gzip, header checksums for tar) and marked intact or damaged; notes, PDFs, pictures, text and Office files open from inside, and any file can be saved. Nothing is extracted to disk. A zipped Goodnotes notebook opens as the notebook; a zip of several notebooks opens as a group of documents. 7-Zip and RAR archives cannot be opened.
                                              
  
                                              Legacy .notecomp and .notepart files also open cleanly here.
