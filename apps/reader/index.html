    Google Drive

    See your Noteful library here. NoteComplete only reads Drive; it never uploads, edits or deletes files.

    1. In Noteful, back up or export your notebooks to a Google Drive folder.
    2. Choose Connect Google Drive and allow read-only access in the Google window.
    3. Open that folder below. It reopens automatically next time, and opened notes are kept on this device.
    Connection setup

    The app owner must enable the Drive API and register this website’s origin in Google Cloud. Enter the public client ID, never a client secret.
